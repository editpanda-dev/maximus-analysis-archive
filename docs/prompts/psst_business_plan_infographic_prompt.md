# PSST 사업계획서용 막시무스 인포그래픽 생성 프롬프트

## 용도

PSST 사업계획서의 문제인식·실현가능성 또는 사업화 전략 섹션에 삽입할 가로형 핵심 서비스 구조 인포그래픽. SVG나 코드 기반 도식이 아니라 고해상도 래스터 이미지로 직접 디자인한다.

## Image generation prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram

Asset type: PSST Korean business plan infographic, high-resolution landscape PNG

Primary request: Design a polished Korean business-plan infographic that explains the MAXIMUS destination recommendation system in one glance. The service starts from a user's real travel constraint and produces purpose-specific destination rankings supported by observed Seoul mobility, commercial district, and card-spending data.

Scene/backdrop: clean off-white business proposal page, no photographic scene, no mockup frame, no browser or phone shell

Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape, generous margins, left-to-right process with five clearly separated stages connected by restrained arrows. Add a short problem statement band at the top and a differentiated outcome band at the bottom. Strong alignment, balanced spacing, suitable for direct placement into a formal Korean government startup plan.

Visual structure:

1. Top problem band
   - Exact headline: "같은 30분, 목적이 달라도 같은 추천?"
   - Exact subtext: "거리순·인기순 추천은 이동 제약과 방문 목적의 차이를 충분히 반영하지 못합니다."

2. Five-stage central flow
   - Stage 1 title: "조건 입력"
   - Stage 1 detail: "회기역 · 대중교통 30분 · 시간대 · 목적"
   - Stage 2 title: "도달 후보 구성"
   - Stage 2 detail: "실제로 갈 수 있는 지역만 선별"
   - Stage 3 title: "목적 적합도 분석"
   - Stage 3 detail: "식사 · 카페 · 공부 · 여가 · 쇼핑 · 문화"
   - Stage 4 title: "실제 행동 검증"
   - Stage 4 detail: "생활이동 · 상권 · 카드소비 데이터"
   - Stage 5 title: "근거 있는 순위"
   - Stage 5 detail: "예상 유입 · 이동시간 · 추천 이유"

3. Small data foundation strip beneath stages 2-4
   - Exact label: "데이터 기반"
   - Three compact pillars: "B078 수도권 이동", "B079 카드소비", "서울 상권·시설"
   - Use small database/map/card icons, not logos

4. Bottom differentiated outcome band
   - Exact headline: "인기 장소 나열이 아닌, 조건별 선택 가능성을 설명"
   - Three short outcomes: "목적별 다른 순위", "숨은 목적지 발견", "추천 근거와 불확실성 제시"

Style/medium: premium editorial infographic, Korean public-sector startup proposal aesthetic, custom raster design, clean flat shapes with subtle depth, restrained rounded cards, thin connector arrows, crisp professional iconography

Color palette: deep navy #173B63, confident blue #2E74B5, teal #2A9D8F, warm accent orange #F2A65A, off-white #F7F8FA, dark charcoal text #20242A. Use orange only for the user problem and key differentiation, teal for data validation, navy and blue for process stages.

Typography: modern Korean sans-serif, strong headline hierarchy, short readable labels, accurate Korean text, no tiny text. Render every quoted Korean phrase verbatim. Keep English limited to B078 and B079.

Icon direction: origin pin and clock for input, transit radius/map for reachable candidates, six-purpose grid for purpose fit, database/card/flow symbols for validation, ranked cards with explanation tags for output

Constraints: direct infographic artwork only; raster PNG; no SVG appearance, no wireframe, no fake app screenshot, no corporate logos, no photographs, no people, no decorative gradients behind text, no excessive shadows, no watermark, no extra text beyond the specified copy, no spelling changes, no illegible microcopy

Avoid: neon colors, playful cartoon styling, 3D isometric city, dense dashboard, clutter, generic AI brain imagery, robot icons, chat bubbles, stock-template appearance

## 검수 항목

- 가로형 16:9 구성인지 확인
- 모든 한글 문구가 정확한지 확인
- 다섯 단계가 왼쪽에서 오른쪽으로 자연스럽게 읽히는지 확인
- 문제–분석–결과 흐름이 한눈에 드러나는지 확인
- PSST 사업계획서에 삽입했을 때 글자가 충분히 읽히는지 확인
- SVG·와이어프레임 느낌이 아닌 완성된 PNG 디자인인지 확인
